3.4.4 Entity Normalisation — recommendation

I would remove this subsection from 3.4.

Your current architecture says:

BioRED -> extraction -> evaluation

CBC -> extraction -> normalisation -> validation -> KG

Therefore NEN is not really part of the extraction pipeline that you are developing/evaluating on BioRED. It is a downstream component specifically involved in constructing the contemporary KG.

I would restructure Section 3.6 as:
